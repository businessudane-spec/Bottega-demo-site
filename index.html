OUR METHODOLOGY

The 4-Stage Architectural Ritual

STAGE 01

Topographical & Climate Reading

We analyze wind vectors, solar geometry, microclimate, and native soil contours to determine building orientation.

STAGE 02

Vernacular Hand-Sketching

Initial spatial volumes are sketched by hand on paper before 3D digital simulation and sun-path mapping.

STAGE 03

Artisanal Material Sourcing

We source local granite, reclaimed teak, and terracotta directly from traditional South Indian craftsmen.

STAGE 04

Precision Handcraft & Delivery

On-site stonemasons and architects execute every joint, courtyard pool, and louvered facade to perfection.
