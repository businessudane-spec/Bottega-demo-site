OUR STORY & PRACTICE

Bottega is an interdisciplinary design practice synthesizing architecture, spatial narratives, and climate sensitivity.

15+ Years of Practice
120+ Realized Buildings
4A Design Honors

Founded in Calicut, Bottega operates across Kerala and Karnataka, bringing together architects, structural thinkers, interior curators, and master stonemasons under a shared ethos of quiet permanence.

Contextual Philosophy

We reject cookie-cutter globalized aesthetics in favor of deeply contextual architecture. Every project begins with an exploration of sun angles, monsoon rainfall patterns, microclimate, and the cultural memory of the region.

Tactile Craftsmanship

Our work celebrates the honesty of raw materials—exposed masonry, hand-chiselled granite, reclaimed teak, and porous laterite stone. We collaborate closely with local artisans who carry generations of craft knowledge.
